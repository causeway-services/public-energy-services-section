Causeway Services' public energy-resource section. It publishes dated records of delivered-energy prices: utility electricity tariffs and regional propane and diesel prices, with their sources, dates, and evidence status preserved on every row.

The retained `project-benchmark-latest` helper projects the newest record into an ES module when a separate bundle consumer needs an embedded benchmark. This section does not operate an HTTP gateway, sell x402 calls, execute trades, or manage an energy hedge.

The observation and transformation capabilities remain owned by the engineering services that produce them. This public section is their discoverable record carrier.
